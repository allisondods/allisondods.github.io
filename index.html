I'm a PhD student in Linguistics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Drs. Colin Phillips and Jeff Lidz. I study psycholinguistics, and I'm primarily interested in how syntactic structure is acquired, represented, and produced.

Before coming to Maryland, I earned my MS in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington with Dr. Emily M. Bender and my BS in Symbolic Systems at Stanford University with Dr. Michael Frank. I also spent some time as a program manager at Microsoft.
